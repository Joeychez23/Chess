Privacy Policy

What this chess site keeps about you, why, and how to delete it.

Who we are

No. 01

This site is run by [OPERATOR NAME]. Questions and requests about your data go to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we keep

No. 02

Your account: your email address, password, screenname, and first and last name, held by our sign in provider, Auth0. We never see your saved password.

Your play: your username, games and moves, rating, default start settings, and friends and friend requests.

Guests: the game you are playing, with no account.

Technical data: your IP address, used to stop abuse, and error reports with the page and your browser's name. Server logs are kept for about 30 days.

Why we keep it

No. 03

To sign you in, run your games, show your rating and friends, keep the site secure, and fix problems. The site has no ads or analytics.

What other players see

No. 04

Your username, your games with them, your rating in those games, and whether you are online to your friends. Friends can find you by your username, and a player who knows your email can invite you with it. Your email itself isn't shown to them.

Who handles it

No. 05

Auth0 handles sign in. Amazon Web Services hosts the site and stores the data. Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, so your browser asks Google for them. We share data with others only when the law requires it.

Cookies & storage

No. 06

Cookies keep you signed in and tie a guest to their game. Your browser also stores a guest's game id and your choices for sound, the evaluation bar and the move list. None of these track you across other sites.

How long we keep it

No. 07

Guests' games and open games are deleted 25 days after the last move. Your account and finished games stay until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

No. 08

Use Delete my account on the settings page. It deletes your sign in, game lists, settings, rating and friends, and frees your username. Your finished friend games stay for your opponents, with your name replaced by "Deleted player" and an internal id that no longer links to an account. Your finished AI games are deleted 25 days later.

Your rights

No. 09

Depending on where you live, you can have the right to see, correct, delete or move your data, and to complain to a regulator. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll answer.

The site isn't meant for children under [MINIMUM AGE]. We'll post any change to this policy here with a new date.
